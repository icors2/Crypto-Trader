# Legal templates

**Not legal advice.** These are placeholders for vibe / early products. Replace every `[PLACEHOLDER]` and have a lawyer review before you rely on them for a real business, regulated data, or paid customers.

| File | Copy to |
| --- | --- |
| `privacy-policy.md` | `app/privacy` page, `content/privacy.md`, or static `/privacy` |
| `terms-of-service.md` | `/terms` |

Link both from the site footer when the app is public. `polish-ui` checks for those links.
